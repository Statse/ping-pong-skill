A pocket tracker for one habit at a time. You define done (e.g. “read 10 pages”), shown beneath the day's only question: “Did you [habit] today?” Answer yes/no in one tap from a widget or an optional silent notification at your chosen time. No app opening needed. After answering, the widget shows today's dot; tapping it opens yes/no for correction, never flips it. At midnight in the phone's current time zone, the day locks; unanswered stays blank. Time-zone changes never reopen locked days or create another question for an existing date. Expired notifications can't save edits. A recent-weeks grid shows yes filled, no hollow, unanswered empty. Switching habits or rewording done archives the old grid read-only; the new habit starts tomorrow. Data stays on your phone; no account, follow-up reminders, streaks or guilt.
