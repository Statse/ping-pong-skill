# One question a day: a pocket habit tracker

**The idea.** One habit at a time, one question a day, answered in a single tap
without opening the app. Nothing nags you, nothing shames you, and a missed day
is simply a blank.

**What you define.** You pick the habit and you write what counts as done — "read
10 pages", not "read more". That definition sits under the daily question so the
answer always means the same thing.

**The daily moment.** At a time you choose, a widget — or an optional silent
notification — asks "Did you [habit] today?" Yes or no, one tap, done. After you
answer, the widget shows today's dot. Tapping that dot reopens the yes/no choice
so you can correct a mistake; it never silently flips your answer.

**The day's edge.** At midnight in the phone's current time zone the day locks.
An unanswered day stays blank rather than counting as a no. Crossing time zones
never reopens a locked day and never produces a second question for a date you
have already answered. A notification you ignored until after midnight cannot
save an answer.

**What you see.** A grid of recent weeks: yes filled, no hollow, unanswered
empty. That is the whole history view — no charts, no streak counter, no score.

**Changing habits.** Switching habits, or rewording what counts as done, archives
the current grid read-only and starts the new habit tomorrow — so you never get a
second question today, and old data is never retroactively reinterpreted.

**Privacy.** The data stays on the phone. No account, no sign-up, no sync.

## Deliberately not included

Follow-up reminders, streaks, guilt mechanics, multi-habit tracking, and any
cloud account. Each of these was considered and rejected during refinement:
the product's value is that it asks once and then leaves you alone.
